# Charter — w05-aduana

> The constitution of this product. When my agent and I disagree about a feature at 2am, this is the referee. Written BEFORE the first line of code.

**What it exists to do:** make a patient-navigation pilot review itself before it betrays the patient — before it signs with a payer (conflicts, data exposure, pharmacy vs remittance incentives) and before each handoff (which door, which documents, what it costs, who owns the next action). Blueprint T2 · Week 5 · Condition 6 first.

**For whom:** the coordinator of a navigation pilot (not a lawyer, reads on her phone) and the named human navigator. Never the patient directly.

**What it will never do:**
- Produce a risk score, a screening, or a diagnosis (Condition 1).
- Let the LLM decide a verdict, invent a clause, or talk to a patient (Conditions 3, 6).
- Store personal data, or accept real patient data as input.
- Tell a navigator to hide a known result to get someone into insurance (Condition 4).
- Sell a "seal" to a sponsor, or let a sponsor pay to change a rule.

**Long view (3 sentences):** In three years Aduana is the review any Mexican navigation or screening pilot runs before accepting money from a pharmacy chain, a remittance group or a store with installment credit — a public, versioned rulebook whose seal a sponsor earns only by signing the curing clauses. Its route map becomes the shared, dated, sourced eligibility map that field navigators maintain instead of each rediscovering the window. Load-bearing walls: public rules, the LLM never decides, no sponsor pays to change a rule, no personal data stored.
